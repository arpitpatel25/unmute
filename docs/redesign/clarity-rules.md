# Landing page clarity rules (agreed 2026-10-10)

Origin: Christian Korb's reply on X, "I've checked your site maybe 10 times now and still have no clear picture on what unmute even is." He thought it was "a super basic IDE sugarcoated with voice". The site must be fixed so that cannot happen again. Every change is checked against this list.

## Words
1. **Literal, no metaphors.** Say what it is, what you do, and what happens. Bad: "a key you hold anywhere", "taps you on the shoulder", "the task comes to you". Good: "Press a key, say what you want done, and Claude Code or Codex starts on it."
2. **Define it right under the headline:** "Unmute is a Mac app…". Every peer site does this (Herdr, Wispr, Underdog).
3. **Name the category and what it is NOT:** "Not an IDE. Not just dictation. It works with the Claude Code and Codex you already pay for."
4. **One vocabulary:** "session" (not task, agent session or notch session). Keys are named once, in the steps.
5. **Name the tools people know:** Claude Code and Codex, by name.

## Structure
6. **Sell one promise:** voice → Claude Code/Codex session from any app → answer when it asks.
7. **Keep features in the product but off the homepage.** Unmute Agent, meeting notes, scratchpad, pocket and screenshot capture are not sections. At most they get one line each in "Also included", and everything goes on a separate "Everything Unmute does" page.
8. **About five sections:**
   1. Headline + definition + what it isn't + hero video
   2. Three steps, each with one short clip
   3. "Not just code" strip
   4. Also included / requirements / price
   5. Download
9. **No philosophy section.** At most one sign-off line at the end.

## Visuals
10. **Real footage only.** Use the Tella recordings; no drawn Mac, fake windows or app-UI animations.
11. **Hero:** real voice plus captions. Autoplays muted, with a clear "Sound on" button.
12. **Every clip has a plain caption** saying what happens.
13. **Calm top:** no moving words behind the headline. The definition must be the most readable thing.
14. **Mobile first-class:** the definition and video work on a phone (most X traffic).

## Consistency
15. **Pricing and Dictation pages must tell the same story,** with the same names and the same product. Today they say "Remote / Orchestrate" and "dictation is the entire product".
16. **Proof as soon as it exists:** real user quotes or numbers.

## Before publishing
17. **5-second test:** show the top screen to 5 Claude Code users who have never seen Unmute and ask "what does this do?". Publish only if 4 of 5 can say it back.
