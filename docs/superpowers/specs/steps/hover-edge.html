A border around the notch — on a black menu bar

Drawn against your menu bar, not a wallpaper. The mass is opaque black and the bar behind it is black, so at rest the surface has no visible edge at all — the first row is not a rendering mistake. Every option below is a way of giving it one.

The constraint, from NotchView: “there is NO rim on the bar-level mass — a stroke around it would outline the black against the housing and put back the join.” That objection is about the middle, where the mass meets the hardware. The outer ends are nowhere near it.
