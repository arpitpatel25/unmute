Three keys, and your hands never leave what you were doing.

Unmute lives in the notch. It does three things, and you drive all of them without opening a window.

One

Dictation that carries more than words

Hold Fn and talk. It types where your cursor already is — a terminal, a notes app, a chat box, anything.

Grab a screenshot part-way through a sentence and it travels with the words. When you let go, the whole thing lands in one paste: what you said and what you showed, together.

Two

Say the work, and it starts

Hold right ⌥ and describe what you want done. It opens a real Claude Code or Codex session — on the plan you already pay for — and gets on with it.

The task waits in the pocket until it needs you. right ⌥ again to reply, left ⌥ to walk between them, or open the Orchestrator and see every one at once.

Three

It takes the meeting notes

left ⌃ twice and it starts listening — your microphone and the room's audio both.

What you get back is notes, not a transcript. Nothing leaves the widget until you end it, and ending is never one click.
