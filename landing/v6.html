Do I need a coding agent?

Yes, for sessions. Unmute starts and answers them in the agents you already have: Claude Code, Codex, Muse, OpenCode, Pi, Grok or Cursor. Dictation, notes and the scratchpad work without them.

Do I have to create or manage bots?

No. Say the task and Unmute puts it in the right bot, or creates a new one when nothing fits.

Does it work in every app?

Yes. Unmute sits on top of your whole Mac, so right ⌥ works in Slack, Chrome, Figma, your editor, anywhere.

Where do my sessions run?

On your Mac, in your own coding agent. You can open any session there and keep going by typing.

Can I run several at once?

Yes. Each request is its own session, and they all show in the notch.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon.
