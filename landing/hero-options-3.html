A · Fan out

The badge splits into three thin branches, each ending in a live session. Every request that arrives travels down one branch and becomes that session's task. Reads as "one voice, many agents" at a glance.

B · Stack

No lines. Each request drops out of the badge as a new session card; the cards stack under it, three at most, the oldest fading away. Quietest of the four.

C · Tree

A line drops from the badge and splits into a row of sessions underneath, like a tree. The request lights up its branch on the way down. Most structured; reads like a diagram.

D · The pocket

The badge opens into the app's own session list, the black pocket from the notch. Each request lands as a new row, Working in green; one of them asks for you in orange. The most "this is the product".
