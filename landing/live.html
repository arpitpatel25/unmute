What happens after you speak.

Scroll through one request, start to finish.

  1. You tap a key and say it.Tap right ⌥ anywhere on your Mac. Your words appear as you speak.
  2. Unmute sends it.To a new Claude Code or Codex session, with what you were looking at.
  3. It works in the background.A small status at the top of your screen. You keep working.
  4. It asks when it needs you.The session opens in the notch, right where you are. No switching windows.
  5. You answer. It’s done.Reply by voice. The result comes back the same way.
