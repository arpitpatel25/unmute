Unmute vs T3 Code

T3 Code gives your agents a window. Unmute lets you talk to Claude Code and Codex from whatever app you’re in, and brings the answer back to you.

Checked Oct 20, 2026 · Sources

vs

Two ways to reach your agents

Two different ways in

A window you go to

You open T3 Code, pick a thread and type. To check on it or answer it, you go back to the window.

An interface that comes to you

You tap right ⌥ in any app and say it. When it’s done or needs you, the chat opens in the notch, right where you are.

The same request, two ways

A bug report comes in while you’re in Slack.

In T3 Code

1 · Switch to T3 Code2 · Pick the agent and the thread3 · Type the request4 · Switch back to Slack5 · Go back later to check on it

With Unmute

1 · Tap right ⌥ in Slack2 · Say “Find what broke checkout and open a PR”3 · Keep working in Slack4 · It opens in the notch when it needs you5 · Answer by voice
