How it works

  1. 1

    Say it from any app.

    Hold right ⌥ and speak. Unmute starts a new Claude Code or Codex session with your words as the prompt, or sends them to a session that’s already running.

  2. 2

    It works in the background.

    The session runs in Claude Code or Codex, on your own subscription. A small status at the top of your screen shows it’s working. You keep doing what you were doing.

  3. 3

    Answer when it asks.

    When a session finishes or needs a decision, it shows at the top of your screen. Read it and reply by voice, from wherever you are.
