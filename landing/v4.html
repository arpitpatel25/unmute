Questions

Short answers.

Do I need Claude Code or Codex?

Yes, for sessions. Unmute starts and answers them in the Claude Code or Codex you already have. Dictation, notes and the scratchpad work without them.

Does it work in every app?

Yes. Right ⌥ works in Slack, Chrome, Figma, your editor, anywhere on your Mac.

Where do my sessions run?

On your Mac, in your own Claude Code or Codex. You can open any session there and keep going by typing.

Can I run several at once?

Yes. Each request is its own session, and they all show in the notch.

Which Macs?

Macs with Apple silicon.

How much is it?

$7.99 a month or $79 a year, with everything included.
