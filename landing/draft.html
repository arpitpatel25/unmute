How it works

  1. 1

    Say what you want done.

    Tap right ⌥ and speak. Your words start a new Claude Code or Codex session, or go to one that’s already running.

  2. 2

    Keep working.

    The session runs in Claude Code or Codex. A small status at the top of your screen shows it’s working.

  3. 3

    Answer when it asks.

    When the session finishes or needs a decision, its chat opens at the top of your screen. Read it and reply by voice, without going anywhere.
