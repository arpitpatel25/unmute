What unmute does

unmute records audio when you press a configured trigger key, transcribes it via one of three engines you choose, and pastes the result into the focused application. The three engines are:

  • On-device — transcription runs locally via whisper.cpp on your Mac.
  • Bring-your-own-key (BYOK) — audio is sent directly from your Mac to Groq using an API key you provide.
  • No key required — audio is sent through our infrastructure to Groq and the transcript is returned. Requires a free account and prepaid credits.
