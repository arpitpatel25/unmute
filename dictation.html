// raw by default — the philosophy

Here's our actual opinion, since you're reading: prompting is what people dictate for now. Most of what you say goes straight into ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor. Prompts. And prompts don't need commas.

So we paste raw — no auto-punctuation, no autocorrect quietly editing you into a stranger. Same accuracy, none of the cleanup, none of the "wait, I didn't say that."

The point: formatting is something you ask for, not something inflicted on you by an overconfident autocorrect.
