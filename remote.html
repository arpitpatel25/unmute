Do I need Claude Code or Codex?

Only for agent-powered work. Remote connects to your own Claude Code or Codex setup. Free local dictation does not need either. Meeting notes use your connected agent to write the summary.

Does it work on a Mac without a notch?

Yes. Unmute provides its own surface at the top of the screen. You do not need a MacBook with a physical display cutout.

Where does my audio go?

In local mode, dictation is processed on your Mac. Managed cloud dictation sends audio through Unmute’s service to a transcription provider. With your own API key, requests go directly to your provider. Agent tasks follow your connected provider’s data handling. Read the privacy policy for details.
