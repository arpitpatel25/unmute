// how it works

  • 1. Speak the task. Hold right ⌥ and say it like a human. No prompt-craft, no syntax, no "as an AI language model."
  • 2. It runs on your Mac. Real files, real terminal, your actual logged-in Chrome, your apps. Not a sandbox. Not a vibe.
  • 3. You watch, or you don't. A live view shows the work; an indicator nags you only when it genuinely needs a decision.
  • 4. It comes back. The result lands where you're working — files, links, the lot. Answer follow-ups by voice.

// you're the driver, not a passenger

To get things done without pestering you about every step, Remote runs with real permissions. The guardrails, because we're not reckless:

  • Human-initiated, always. It does the one thing you asked, when you ask. No background agent quietly redecorating your hard drive.
  • Kill it instantly. One stop, everything stops.
  • Training wheels, optional. Turn on per-action approval, or fence it to a single folder.
  • Your credentials stay yours. We never see your keys or logins — you authorize things inside your own Claude Code. Frankly we don't want them; they sound like a liability.
