Questions

Short answers about setup, agents and price.

Ask us on X →
Do I need Claude Code or Codex?

Yes. Unmute starts and answers sessions in the Claude Code or Codex you already have, with your own plan. Dictation, the notetaker and the scratchpad work without them.

Does it work in every app?

Yes. Unmute sits on top of your whole Mac, so right ⌥ works in Slack, Chrome, Figma, your editor, anywhere.

Where do my sessions run?

On your Mac, in your own Claude Code or Codex. You can open any session there and carry on by typing.

Can I run several sessions at once?

Yes. Each request is its own session. They all show in the notch, and you can talk into any of them.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon.

How much is it?

$7.99 a month or $79 a year, with everything included.
