unmute is a Mac dictation app. You hold a key and talk; text appears where your cursor is.

Then we taught it to do more than type. Typing sucks. So we made it optional.

// what it is

Hold Fn, talk, release. Your words land at the cursor — Slack, email, code, anywhere. That's the whole product. It took two years. You're welcome.

// why it's the fastest one

  • ~1 second. Always. Not "usually." Not "when the API feels like it."
  • 10 seconds of audio or 10 minutes — same ~1 second. Latency doesn't grow with your monologue.
  • Cloud when your Wi-Fi behaves, on-device the instant it doesn't. You'll never watch a spinner eat your words.

Wispr Flow times out and loses the lot. We just… don't. the receipts

// raw by default

We paste exactly what you said. No auto-punctuation, no "corrections" you have to undo. Half of it goes into ChatGPT anyway, and it does not care about your commas.

Want it formatted? Tap Caps Lock and ask — "clean this up," "three bullets," "less corporate." On demand, not by force.

// unmute remote — the sequel

Dictation types. Remote does the thing.

"Extract that zip." "Reply to that email." "Put on the next episode." It runs on your actual Mac — your files, terminal, browser, apps — and reports back. Hold right ⌥, say it, done.
