Questions

Do I need a Claude Code subscription for Remote?

Yes. Remote runs Claude Code on your own machine, so it uses your existing Claude Code plan. unmute is the voice layer that finally lets you spend what you're already paying for.

Is the free version crippled?

No — on-device dictation is genuinely useful and fully offline. The subscription adds cloud speed and Remote, which need our infrastructure and your Claude Code, respectively.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel whenever — see the refund policy for details.

What about my privacy?

On-device dictation never leaves your Mac. For Remote, unmute never handles your credentials — you authorize integrations in your own Claude Code. See the privacy policy.
