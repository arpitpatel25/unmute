Talk to the founder ↗
Do I need an account to start?

No. On-device dictation is free and runs without an Unmute account. Sign in if you choose a managed cloud subscription.

Do I need Claude Code or Codex?

Only for agent-powered work. Remote connects to your own Claude Code or Codex setup. Free local dictation does not need either. Meeting notes use your connected agent to write the summary.

How do I subscribe or cancel?

Download Unmute and open Account to choose a plan and billing interval. Manage your subscription from the app’s billing settings. Cancellation stops future renewals; see the terms and refund policy for details.

Are agent costs included?

No. Remote uses your connected Claude Code or Codex account. Their subscription, API, and usage charges are separate from Unmute.
